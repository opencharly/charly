# 2026.230.0818 — provenance out of the skills, and one hedge that had already gone false

Executes the "skill prose: provenance out, incidents stay" batch registered with the
generate-packages shim, and removes the registration — the batch is finished, not re-homed.

Registering it was the right call at the time and the wrong thing to leave standing: its
owner field named the nFPM program, which has now completed every phase, so the batch named
as its owner a program with no remaining work. That is how a registered batch decays back
into the deferral it replaced.

## The find that justifies the sweep

`/charly-internals:vm-deploy-target` carried a temporal hedge that had **already decayed to
false**:

> There is deliberately **no fallback** for either value — as of this cutover, whose spec and
> sdk legs are unmerged at the time of writing; on released charly the field is still
> `distro?: string` with the inference in place.

Both halves are wrong at HEAD. `spec/schema/vm.cue` declares `distro?: #DistroID` — the
vocabulary type, not `string` — and `spec/spec/vocab_gen.go` carries the generated
`DistroSSHUnits` / `DistroInits` tables the same paragraph reads from, so those legs are
merged. A hedge written to be careful became the only false sentence on the page, and nothing
re-checks a hedge. The paragraph now states the rule in the present tense.

## Swept

- **`charly-internals`** — the decayed hedge above, plus "An interim revision of this skill
  claimed the sshd start falls back…". The rule survives stated forward: the start does NOT
  fall back, because a fallback lets a wrong input survive long enough to fail elsewhere.
- **`charly-build`** — "An earlier revision of this list named six of the twelve…" becomes
  the standing hazard: a partial list here is worse than silence, and the way it happens is a
  windowed `grep` published as complete.
- **`charly-hooks`** — "an earlier revision of this comment was not [precise]" becomes the
  measurement it was hedging: the script DOES block what it is meant to catch.
- **`shortbread`** ×2 — "as of 2026-Q1" becomes "shipped by CachyOS / Arch". The Boost fact
  is the content; the quarter is when someone looked.

## Deliberately NOT swept

- **`charly-internals` "An earlier revision said 'every factual claim in the three MECHANISM
  sections…'"** — this is an incident that JUSTIFIES a rule. It explains why the execution
  rule is scoped to CONTENT rather than to sections: the narrower wording exempted a broken
  shell recipe sitting in an epistemic section. Delete the story and the scoping looks
  arbitrary.
- **Bare `RDD-confirmed` markers.** They carry no date and assert that a claim was proven
  empirically, which is a fact about the claim's standing, not about when someone looked.
- **`selkies`'s "a fork not yet merged upstream"** and **MCP protocol versions.** Facts about
  the world that explain a current design, not provenance of the text.

The distinction the batch encodes, now applied and retired: **a date says WHEN someone learned
a fact and decays; a measurement or a citation says WHAT IS TRUE and is the content.**

# 2026.240.1711 — feat(init): render the portable lifecycle fields and unit_options

## Summary

The charly (template-data) leg of batch **B2**, and the one that makes the whole batch reach an
emitted unit. `spec#58` made the fields authorable, `spec#59` carried them into the render context,
`plugin-init#3` added the flattener — none of it produced a single line of output until the templates
read them.

**systemd** now emits `Type=`, `Requires=`, `RestartSec=`, `WatchdogSec=` and every
`unit_options.systemd` directive. `Type=` falls back to `simple` when unset, so an entry declaring
nothing renders exactly as before.

**OpenRC** gains the same fields in its own spelling: `need <unit>` inside the `depend()` block it
already emits for `localmount`, `respawn_delay`, `healthcheck_timer`, and its own `unit_options` key.

[Unit]

[Service]

Restart=always
RestartSec=5s

RuntimeDirectory=cstream

Two things to note. The list expands to **two** `RuntimeDirectory=` lines — a scalar collapse would
have emitted one unusable value. And the same input carried an `openrc` key in `unit_options`, of
which **nothing** reached the systemd unit.

Both are already-merged, already-tagged sibling legs. Those spec releases exist only because

Go-module form, so its releases had silently stopped at `v0.2026232.520` and **no consumer could name
a version containing these schema changes**.

## A second stub, and why it is a stub

`TestEmbeddedVocab_OpenRCIsAFirstClassInit` parses the OpenRC template with an explicit func map, and
`initDirectives` is the second custom function to appear there after `openrcLog`. It is **stubbed,
not reimplemented**: the flattening semantics are owned by `candy/plugin-init`'s `initDirectives` and
pinned by its `TestInitDirectivesFlattensUnitOptions`. A second copy here could drift from the one
the renderer actually uses, and the test's purpose is "the shipped template parses and renders", not
"the flattening is correct".

## Not covered

- **B2's third item, `wait_for:`**, remains open and is the batch's last piece. Nine hand-rolled
  `while [ ! -e "$SOCKET" ]` loops exist today (labwc, kde-selkies ×2, chrome-cdp, wayvnc ×3, swaync,
  waybar, waybar-labwc, sway), each with a hardcoded timeout that R4 forbids. Each init needs a real
  lowering — systemd `ExecStartPre=`, supervisord a rendered pre-exec, OpenRC `depend(){ need … }` —
  and the honest gate is deleting that duplication with the affected beds staying green, which is a
  cutover of its own rather than a rider on this one.
- **No bed deploys systemd or OpenRC**, so `Requires=`/`RestartSec=`/`WatchdogSec=` are proven to
  *render*, not to *take effect*. That is the same gap the cstream VM bed exists to close.

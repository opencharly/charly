## The real find: a gate that could not reach its own assertions

`TestContractPinsMatchShared` took the **first** `opencharly/*` contract require in `charly/go.mod`
as its spec anchor. That was `spec` — until `sdk` became an **indirect** require of core and sorted
above it. From then on the anchor was silently `sdk`, and the test fataled on a tree whose pins were
entirely correct:

```
--- FAIL: TestContractPinsMatchShared
    ../../charly/go.mod: no `github.com/opencharly/spec <version>` require found
```

`spec` was right there at line 132. Both contracts are now looked up **by name**, and the `sdk` pin
is anchored from core exactly as `spec` is, replacing a hardcoded constant that had itself drifted
(`v0.2026234.347` against the tree's `v0.2026241.1032`).

Repairing the anchor let the gate reach its real assertions **for the first time**, and it
immediately caught what it was written to catch:

- **`tools/golden-compile/go.mod` carried a committed absolute-path `replace`:**
  ```
  replace github.com/opencharly/spec => /home/atrawog/Sync/Atrapub/coder/pi/opencharly/spec
  ```
  pointing into a personal checkout — making that module unbuildable for anyone else, on any other
  machine. Post-cutover **no** in-repo module may replace a contract module; both resolve from the
  proxy. Removed.
- **Three modules sat on stale contract pins** (`candy/generate-packages`, `tools/golden-cloudinit`,
  `tools/golden-compile`). All advanced to the shared pins and `go mod tidy`'d.

`TestCandyGoModsAreCanonical` is deleted rather than repointed: it globbed `candy/plugin-*/go.mod`
and `t.Fatal`'d on an empty match, but those modules live in their own repos with their own CI, so
the assertion moved with them. Repointing it at nothing would be worse than removing it.

# 2026.243.0628 — chore: advance box/cachyos to the GPU-bed snapshot

## Summary

Advances `box/cachyos` to `distro-cachyos` HEAD, adopting the snapshot that landed in
`distro-cachyos#54`:

- **`check-cstream-gpu-vm`** — the GPU venue for cstream, and the measurement its encoder contract
  rests on. The plan asserts *"a passed-through 4080 has no VA `EncSlice`"* and builds three later
  claims on it; that premise had never been measured, and is now **asserted** by the bed rather than
  assumed.
- **`btrfs-progs`** on `cachyos-vm` and `cachyos-gpu-vm` — both have a btrfs rootfs and no fsck
  helper, so `mkinitcpio` warns *"No fsck helpers found"*, its warnings escalate to *"errors were
  encountered during the build"* → `error: command failed to execute correctly`, and charly's
  log-diagnostic gate fails the step **even though `deploy-add` itself exits 0**.

## Why this needs a PR at all

Policy B pins the umbrella's `distro-*` to *exactly the commits charly's own gitlinks pin* — the
umbrella means "the org as charly sees it". So `distro-cachyos` does **not** reach the umbrella on
its own merge: charly has to adopt the snapshot first. And unlike the umbrella, charly has no sync
workflow (`ci`, `pr-validator`, `release-binary`, `tag-on-merge` only), so the adoption is a
deliberate, manual step. Without it the GPU bed sits merged and unreachable.

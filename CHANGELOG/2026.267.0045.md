## Summary

Bump the **welded** `plugin-review` pin from `v2026.265.0505` → **`v2026.266.1944`** so the next charly release's plugin bundle carries the per-file patch recovery (merged `opencharly/plugin-review#21`, tag `v2026.266.1944`).

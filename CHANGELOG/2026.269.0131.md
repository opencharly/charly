### Why the pin bumps

Charly core compiles `plugin-substrate` and `plugin-egress` in. The pins inherited
from main **predate this cutover**: `plugin-substrate@v0.2026268.2319` has no
`kindcluster` word, and `plugin-egress@v0.2026250.951` has no `kind_cluster`
schema. Without bumping them the substrate would never resolve and the
deploy:kindcluster preresolve would fail its egress gate with "no egress schema
registered". Both target tags are merged; `plugin-kube` (the `deploy:kindcluster`
provider) is loaded out-of-process via the `check-kindcluster` bed's `add_candy:`
at its merged tag.

### The bed uses `nodes`/`wait-nodes`/`storageclass`/`raw` — **not `addons`**

`kube: addons` waits on the k3s addon stack (Traefik / ServiceLB / local-path in
`kube-system`). kind ships **kindnet** and **no Traefik/ServiceLB**, and its
local-path provisioner lives in the `local-path-storage` namespace with the
StorageClass named `standard`. The bed asserts the kind-appropriate facts.

### Packaging

`kind` is packaged in **Arch `extra`** (so it is a hard `depends` there, plus its
`containerd` rootless-nerdctl prerequisite); no native package exists for
rpm/deb/apk, where it is a `suggest` and the `layer-kind` candy downloads the
pinned binary. This is the operator's explicit "add the tools to the charly
package dependencies" requirement.

## 1. The plugin-loader defect (charly core — a real product defect)

`pluginAlreadyConnected` treated a plugin candy's provider list as **all-or-nothing**:
if ANY ONE declared word was registered as a **compiled-in** provider (origin
`builtin`), it returned `connected=true` and the WHOLE candy was skipped. But
`registry_bootstrap.go` documents that every substrate structural kind — including
`kind:kubevirt` — is a compiled-in provider (`candy/plugin-substrate`). So:

- `plugin-kubevirt` declares `kind:kubevirt` (compiled-in) **plus** its OWN
  `verb:kubevirt` / `deploy:kubevirt` / `command:kubevirt`;
- the all-or-nothing rule skipped the whole candy as "already compiled in";
- its `verb:kubevirt` therefore never connected → `kubevirt:` resolved
  **"no provider registered for plugin verb"**.

`plugin-kube` declares no structural kind, so nothing was builtin and it loaded
normally — which is exactly why the `kube:` probes worked all along while the
`kubevirt:` probes failed.

**Root fix — keyed on the WORD's actual serving origin, never a whole-candy "any one":**

- **`pluginAlreadyConnected`** — a candy is already loaded iff (a) ANY declared word is
  registered from the **same source** (loadPluginUnit connects a unit's providers
  atomically, so one same-source word proves the source is loaded), or (b) EVERY
  declared word is a compiled-in word. A candy with at least one not-yet-served word
  loads, so `plugin-kubevirt` connects its own words while its compiled-in
  `kind:kubevirt` is skipped.
- **`register`** — a registration that does not change a provider IDENTITY's placement
  is a no-op, not a collision: an out-of-process registration of an identity already
  owned by a **builtin**, or a **same-origin** re-registration (the same unit loaded
  twice). A DIFFERENT-origin collision and a BUILTIN-vs-BUILTIN duplicate still fail
  fast (the latter panics at `init()`), preserving the startup bijection invariant.

The "same source ⇒ loaded" half also fixes a **second** latent defect the first cut
exposed: a candy scanned under TWO keys (short `plugin-mcp` + source-keyed) whose
`charly.yml` declares a word its served unit does not actually register
(`command:mcp`) was re-loaded by a naive per-word rule, colliding on its
already-registered `verb:mcp` and failing the whole deploy. Same-source detection makes
that re-load a no-op.

## 2. The `check-kubevirt-operator` bed

- **`check-kubevirt-operator`** — a disposable libvirt Arch **k3s VM** (the shared
  `k3s-vm` entity + `k3s-server`, an auto-allocated host→guest 6443 forward) composed
  with `layer-kubevirt-operator` (KubeVirt + CDI) and `layer-kubevirt` (`virtctl`); its
  `add_candy:` names the two out-of-process verb plugins the plan dispatches
  (`plugin-kube` for `kube:`, `plugin-kubevirt` for `kubevirt:`).
- **`check-kubevirt-operator-ctx`** — the bed's OWN per-deploy-domain `kind: kubernetes`
  profile (the task-#18 pattern; the bed shares the `k3s-vm` entity). Every probe names
  it by literal string (`cluster: check-kubevirt-operator-ctx`) — the proven
  `check-k3s-vm` convention.
- **The plan's 7 checks** (`kvo-*`): node Ready; the `virtualmachines.kubevirt.io` and
  `virtualmachineinstances.kubevirt.io` CRDs Established (one raw probe each);
  `virt-handler` DaemonSet Ready; the **KubeVirt CR** and the **CDI CR** reporting
  `Deployed` via the `kubevirt: wait-ready` platform arm (namespace `kubevirt` / `cdi`);
  and the installed `virtctl` client version.

The platform INSTALL runs **in-venue** (`layer-kubevirt-operator`'s `run:` steps against
the guest's own kubeconfig — the `layer-helm-chart` precedent); the probes run
**host-side** over the managed port-forward, on the side that can name a per-deploy
profile. That partition is the plan's WS-7.

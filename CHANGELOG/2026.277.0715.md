# 2026.277.0715 — chore: re-pin sdk to v0.2026277.352 (tolerate an absent substrate ledger on a first deploy)

# Summary

Re-pins charly's `github.com/opencharly/sdk` require from `v0.2026276.1822` to
**`v0.2026277.352`** — the tag-on-merge tag for `opencharly/sdk#343`.

**What the pin buys.** `charly deploy add` into a *newly created* VM hard-failed before this
pin. Two `Via` guards in sdk's `kit/install_ledger.go` treated *any* read error of the
target's venue ledger as fatal, including the not-found case — but a fresh guest legitimately
has no `~/.config/charly/charly.yml` yet. sdk#343 narrows both guards to the not-found case,
and deletes `deploykit`'s duplicate `isMissingArtifactFile` in favour of the newly exported
`kit.IsNotFound`.

**Scope.** `go.mod`/`go.sum` version swaps only, across all four lockstep modules
(`charly/`, `tools/generate-packages`, `tools/golden-cloudinit`, `tools/golden-compile`). No
Go code, docs, workflow, or config-with-logic. 8 files, 12 insertions / 12 deletions.

**One atomic commit.** The require bump and the R5 prune of the retired `v0.2026276.1822`
sum lines land *together*, so no intermediate revision of this branch carries the retired
hashes.

**Deliberately not claimed.** This pin unblocks `charly#730`'s `check-kubevirt-vm`
**`deploy-add` step** only. That bed still fails later, at `bring-up-members`, on a separate
producer defect — `opencharly/plugin-vm#68` (`charly vm build --container-disk` emits a
containerDisk KubeVirt cannot boot: missing `/usr/bin/container-disk`). This PR does **not**
make that bed green, and does not depend on it.

**Supersedes #782 and #784.** Both carried this identical change and both are blocked by a
body/evidence defect rather than by the change: #782's body predated the R5 prune, and #784
now cannot pass at all, because its two non-changelog commits (`26130369` re-pin, then
`af0195f7` prune) split the atomic change, and collapsing a pushed branch would require a
force-push, which R6 forbids. Both predecessors' findings are fixed here in **one** commit.

## Why it is needed now

`opencharly/opencharly#403` replaces the umbrella `verify` task's hand-rolled checkout loop —
the R3 duplicate that kept reintroducing the charly#337 walk-up bug — with
`verb:git-submodules {mode: verify, pin_map: {}}`. That verb is served by the plugin-task
**compiled into the charly binary**: charly's own `charly.yml:3389` calls it "plugin-task,
compiled in" and `:3543` "the compiled-in `candy/plugin-task`", and the umbrella's `charly.yml`
imports only `charly: ./charly` and declares no candies of its own, so the `plugin-task/`
submodule it records is a pin record, not a load path. `charly task sync` therefore cannot bring
this fix — only a require bump can.

Measured on the umbrella head against the pin resolved today (its own `task verify`, 425
submodules):

```
[pass] check — every submodule is clean and checked out at their recorded gitlink — …
    verify: 0 policy pin(s) hold across 425 submodule(s)
```

No checkout clause at all: with `pin_map: {}` the pre-audit verb asserts *nothing*, so
composing it there would delete the checkout audit outright. This PR is what makes that
composition honest, and `#403` does not open until it lands.

Closes `opencharly/charly#817`.

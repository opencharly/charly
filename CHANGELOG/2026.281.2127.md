Consumes **`opencharly/sdk#361`** (MERGED; tag `v0.2026281.2051`, commit `54c82a9`). **Closes `opencharly/charly#847`.**

## The second declaration, routed to its named row (not parked)

**The umbrella `sdk` gitlink is not advanced by this PR, and that surface is now a named row: `opencharly/charly#864`.**

It is not a "separate step": `charly task sync` requires **all 423 submodules to be initialized checkouts** because it walks every gitlink, and in a fresh worktree it fails -

```
bumped 1 submodule pin(s): charly; 422 FAILED:
  submodule "action-review" is not an initialized checkout
```

That is measured and pasted, with candidate fixes, in **`#864`**. It is also *the third surface of the same root condition*: the loader (`opencharly/sdk#361`, consumed here by `#863`, tracked as `opencharly/charly#847`), the umbrella **pre-commit hook** (a single `git submodule update --init --depth 1 charly` makes it pass), and now `task sync`.

**Why this PR is still the right unit:** it is the **module-requirement** half - the declaration the *build* reads, and the one that makes charly's binary carry the fixed loader, which is what closes `#847`. Advancing the gitlink needs an initialized checkout and belongs with `#864`'s fix, not smuggled in here.

# 2026.296.2303 — feat(pins): install the pin-currency control and advance box/ubuntu to the served ref

Closes #1048

## Summary

Head `f6c6ec2a32068f4ce60f6ddfe695570b8fe8481f` — 8 files changed, 604 insertions(+), 2 deletions(-).

`charly`'s own gate never asked the currency question over `charly`'s own `box/*` leaves, so the #1035/#1048 recurrence kept landing: a merge upstream, and no gate anywhere in this repo that asked whether the pin still pointed at it. This installs the control, and advances `box/ubuntu` in the same cutover — the two things #1048 asks for.

**The control.** `charly task pin-current` asks whether every `box/*` gitlink is at the ref this repo's own `.gitmodules` declares for it, and fails only on NEGLECT: a pin that is behind AND that this repo has not advanced for longer than its own bound (168 h). "Behind" alone is churn. `charly task pin-currency-proof` proves the control can fail — it runs the shipped wiring, extracted from this repo's own `charly.yml`, over a scratch fixture whose two arms differ only in the date of the commit that recorded the pin: a neglected pin goes red, the same pin advanced recently passes.

**The correction to the criterion (R4).** #1048 asks for "a gate step in `charly` that fails when a `box/*` twin is behind the ref its own `.gitmodules` declares". Measured over this repo's own history, that gate would be red on 28 of the last 32 days — each of the six twins is behind the ref it declares on 17 to 27 of those days, and on 28 of the 32 at least one is. That is the permanently-red gate the org's own pin rule forbids, so the shipped criterion is narrower, and says so in the file: behind AND un-advanced past this repo's own bound. The bound is derived from this repo's own advance history — the longest gap that stayed within a 7-day bound was 6.4 d, 25 gaps ran past it (8.0 d to 25.1 d), and 168 h (7 d) is the only whole-day bound strictly between those two. `scripts/measure-pin-cadence.py` re-derives every one of those numbers, over a window named by its dates. Over the 9 days from 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-23 the shipped criterion is red on 6, at ages of 7.5 d to 14.7 d — every one inside a real lapse (8.1 d, 10.2 d or 15.3 d), and no red day on a healthy tree.

**The correction to the advance target (R4).** #1048 names `871cb35ea5e7` for `box/ubuntu`. That is no longer the ref `distro-ubuntu`'s served `main` carries — the served tip is `028fd26e7ccadef3774c98b6d7e2e744d1d60faa` — so advancing to the SHA in the issue would have landed the very defect the issue is about, one commit later. The advance therefore goes through `charly task sync` and lands at the served ref, and the umbrella's `distro-ubuntu` edge, blocked on this gitlink, is unblocked for the umbrella's next wave.

**The files.** `.github/workflows/pin-currency.yml` (new) is the control's gate: a `pull_request` trigger for the answer while the change is open, and a daily schedule for the merge that happened with no PR at all. `charly.yml` carries the `STALE_AFTER` parameter, the `pin-current` wrapper, the `pin-currency-proof` task, and the bed step that runs the proof end to end through `verb:task`. `scripts/check-pin-currency.sh` is the control's own proof; `scripts/measure-pin-cadence.py` re-derives the bound. `AGENTS.md` states the control and the measurement. `charly/go.mod` moves `plugin-task` to the release that carries the opt-in `stale_after` bound the control reads. `box/ubuntu` moves `f23f751` → `028fd26e`.

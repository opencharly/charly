# 2026.297.0121 — chore(pins): advance the box/cachyos and box/fedora twins to their served refs

## Summary

This repo's `box/*` twins were behind the refs their own `.gitmodules` declares, and
the advance is this repo's own sanctioned verb — `charly task sync`, the `pins` task
with `MODE=bump`.

Fixes #1053.

| file | what changed |
|---|---|
| `box/cachyos` | the gitlink advanced `42a3f078ac` -> `1893455d3a` |
| `box/fedora` | the gitlink advanced `b3c35d4de6` -> `ee558d4d97` |

Both were behind: each recorded pin sat two commits below the tip its twin's remote
serves. The other four twins — `box/arch`, `box/debian`, `box/omarchy` and
`box/ubuntu` — were already at their served refs, and they did not move.

**Why it matters outside this repo.** The umbrella's `distro-cachyos` and
`distro-fedora` are `derived-from` **edges**, and an edge's value IS its source's
recorded gitlink — which is these two gitlinks. While they were behind, the
umbrella's own currency report named both edges `blocked-by charly:box/cachyos
(behind)` and `blocked-by charly:box/fedora (behind)`, and **no command in the
umbrella could move them**, because the edge takes the source's value. This is the
producer leg of that sequence (opencharly/charly#1053); the umbrella's next pin wave
advances the two edges, through the umbrella's own `charly task sync`.

**What this change is not.** It is not a hand-pin: the verb fetched the twins'
remotes and recorded what they serve, and a hand-pinned SHA cannot be judged, so it
would be a failure. It is not a branch checkout used as a pin. It is not a new pin
and not a new submodule: both paths are already declared in `.gitmodules`.

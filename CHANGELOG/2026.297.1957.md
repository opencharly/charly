This is the producer-first leg of a two-repo chain. `opencharly/plugin-task`'s own coherence control (`scripts/check-coherence.sh`, run by that repo's `coherence` CI job on every PR) asserts that **this repo's** `sdk`/`spec` pins LEAD that module's requires, reading this repo's `main` over HTTP. The consumer leg therefore cannot raise its own `require` until this repo's pin has landed, and the order is fixed by that contract rather than by preference:

1. **this repo** adopts `sdk v0.2026297.1851` — this PR, `opencharly/charly#1062` (issue `opencharly/charly#1061`);
2. `opencharly/plugin-task` raises its `require` to the same version and implements the consumer leg — `opencharly/plugin-task#34`;
3. **this repo** adopts the new `plugin-task` tag, which carries the consumer;
4. the umbrella advances its `charly` and `plugin-task` gitlinks.

### RCA

- **Mechanism:** the pin is a version pin, and the SDK ships its capability in a version. A capability that lands in the SDK is unreachable by a consumer until the consumer's build graph contains it, and the consumer's build graph is dominated by the host's pin.
- **Missed control:** nothing asserts that a landed SDK capability is ADOPTED. The coherence control asserts the opposite direction — that the host's pin is not BEHIND the consumer's require — so a host that has not yet adopted a release is exactly the state it permits. A capability can sit in the SDK, released and tagged, with no consumer able to reach it, and nothing says so.
- **Blast radius:** every consumer of a capability the pinned SDK version lacks. Here: the DATA channel (a task's resolved params cannot reach a step's process environment) and the never-hang verdict (a step killed by its own bound can report a verdict it never produced).
- **Root fix:** adopt the release, and let the coherence control keep the two repos' graphs in step from there.

# 2026.303.0223 — fix(core): the shared rule's READER SET stops being counted by hand (#1096)

## Summary

The shared rule `scripts/tracked-files.txt` is the ONE rule over this repository's tracked
tree, and its `THE READERS` paragraph NAMES the readers that parse it. That paragraph is the
set's ONE home — but ten live sites restated the set's SIZE by hand ("the two gates", "both
of the readers", "BOTH read this file"), and two of the ten were added by opencharly/charly#1095
itself, whose body claimed that *"no surface carries a number a later edit has to update by
hand"*. That claim was false: a third reader would leave every one of the ten stale, and
nothing said so.

This is opencharly/charly#1096, filed from the `charly/pr-validator`'s review of
opencharly/charly#1095, and this PR implements it.

**The RCA.**

- **Mechanism.** The rule file's `THE READERS` paragraph is a derived fact's HOME: it names
  the readers, so the set's SIZE can be read off it. Ten live sites did not read it — they
  stated the size by hand, and a hand-stated size of a derived set is a copy of it (R2).
  #1095's own sweep was keyed on the ARM counts only, so it proved that claim's first half
  and said nothing about its second.
- **Missed control.** Nothing controlled a hand-restated COUNT of the reader set. The ONE
  HOME arm controls the rule's GRAMMAR (one home), its exempt-mode LIST (one home) and the
  reader set (every NAMED reader still READS the rule file) — but a prose numeral that
  counts the set is none of those: no arm reads it, and no claim-keyed sweep was aimed at
  it.
- **Blast radius.** This repository's design keeps growing controls over the tracked tree,
  so a third reader is a normal future edit. Every one of the ten sites would go stale, and
  the sentences that carry the INVARIANT (*the readers cannot drift on WHICH modes are
  exempt*) would still read true — so nothing would say so.
- **Root fix.** DELETE the numeral, do not guard it. A control for a prose numeral is new
  machinery (R3), and prose is the claim-keyed sweep's job (R5). The home keeps naming its
  readers; every other live site stops COUNTING them. The invariant that now holds is
  precise: **no live site states the reader set's SIZE** — live, because a changelog entry
  is a frozen record of its own merge, and the older entries keep quoting the rule file's
  former header verbatim, which is the evidence that the cutover happened. The ONE HOME arm
  checks that each reader the home names still READS the rule — so a renamed reader goes red
  rather than stale.

**What landed.**

- The COUNT is gone from every live site that restated it — the rule file's own `THE READERS`
  paragraph, this repository's `AGENTS.md`, the retired-knob reader's three comment sites,
  the control's module comment and three docstrings, and the CI workflow's header (which
  also stopped counting the AGENTS.md claim-maker bullets — a second derived fact, counted
  by hand in the same hunk, and not one of the ten).
- The self-test's fixture reader is CHECKED against the home instead of hardcoded beside it:
  the rule file's readers paragraph NAMES the readers, and the fixture must be one it names.
- The merged entry `CHANGELOG/2026.303.0035.md` is corrected IN PLACE at SIX sites — one
  errata clause each, carrying SEVEN quoted NUMERALS: the Summary's own reader count quotes
  two, "six arms, one per mutation" quotes one, "the two that were the gate script's own"
  quotes one, "the four surfaces" quotes one in each of its two places, and "the four prose
  surfaces" quotes one — and ONE FALSE CLAIM (the universal *"no surface carries a number a
  later edit has to update by hand"*, replaced by the scoped *"no live site states the reader
  set's SIZE"*, with the scope's reason stated in place); and the disposition of all SIX of
  the review's notes is recorded in a `## Errata` section, the way the org corrected the 1601
  entry (`a51bac14`).

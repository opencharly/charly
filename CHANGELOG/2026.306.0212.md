**Why the plugin-build pin matters.** `v0.2026305.2204` (#42) resolves an imported `arch` intermediate from the charly image store instead of re-pulling it from ghcr — the original blocker recorded on opencharly/.github#263. The sdk bump is a root requirement, so MVS also selects `v0.2026305.2312` for the compiled-in plugin-build packages; the two pins together are what the live build below exercises.

No Go source changed. 12 files: `charly/go.mod` + `go.sum`, three `tools/*` lockstep pairs, four `box/*` gitlinks.

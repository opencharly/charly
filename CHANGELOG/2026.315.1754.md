# 2026.315.1754 — test: model an observing kube method in the external-verb dispatch fixture (#1117)

## Summary

`charly`'s external-verb dispatch fixture modelled `{"method": "apply"}` for the `kube:` verb, and two assertions on the decoded input followed it. `apply` and `delete` — and the `manifest` field that existed only to carry them — were deleted from the verb in **plugin-kube#33** (squash `d19e95d1cad420f152a1e983430d437269b9f8d3`, tag `v2026.315.0153`, closing plugin-kube#32), because DESIGN.md §23 D-VERB-1 says *a verb observes and never changes state*. The served `#KubeInput` now admits **12** method values — 11 observations plus the plugin-internal `k3s-post-provision` — and refuses all three retired identifiers, so the fixture's payload was a shape no author could write and the plugin would reject at load:

### Why the four move TOGETHER — the silent-loss window, measured

`charly#1126` made the gate resolve a kind's value def the way the provider declares it: the provider's `InputDef` FIRST, `spec.KindValueDefs` as the compatibility fallback. So a tree that pinned the new `spec` (no `pod` entry) beside an OLD `plugin-substrate` (no declaration) would fall back to a map entry that no longer exists and **gate nothing, silently**. This leg is the one that closes that window: `plugin-substrate` at its merged tag declares the def, so the fallback is never what answers.

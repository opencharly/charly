# 2026.318.0058 — feat(plugin): the connected plugin record carries the words it serves

## Summary

The loader registers a unit's providers when it connects it — and the manifest a delivered plugin tree needs is built from exactly those words (`spec.PluginManifest`, adopted in `spec v0.2026317.2143`). Until now the connected record carried `key`/`leaf`/`view_path`/`src_dir` and dropped the words, so a delivering step had to re-derive "what does this plugin serve" — and the measured defect (`charly <word>` answering with usage text while every file was present) is what happens when it cannot.

Two lines of behaviour, plus the assertions:

- **`recordConnectedPluginFromLoad` takes the words and stores them** in `spec.DeployConnectedPlugin.Providers`.
- **The words come from `unitProviderKeys(unit)`** — the unit the engine CONNECTED, rendered by the ONE identity renderer (`providerIdentity`/`providerKey`) the rest of the loader already uses. Nothing scrapes the plugin's binary, nothing re-parses its candy, and there is no second spelling of a capability word.
- `go.mod`/`go.sum` — adopt `github.com/opencharly/spec v0.2026317.2143` (the field's wire home) and `github.com/opencharly/sdk v0.2026317.2240` (the released repoint of the manifest writer, sdk#506).

The tests assert the field at BOTH ends: the registry entry (the word list, in order) and the step input the `construct-step` seam injects (the entry's `providers` array), because a record that loses the field in transit is exactly the class this cutover exists to remove.

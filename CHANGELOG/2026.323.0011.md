**What the bump delivers.** plugin-build#60 splits the two tag roles the resolve always had.
`borrowedTag` — `buildkit.ComputeCalVer()`, computed once per resolve — stays on every box the
build does **not** produce: a builder borrowed from another repository's namespace, and any
auto-intermediate off a target's own chain. `targetTag` — the caller's `req.Tag` from
`charly box build <box> --tag <t>` — is stamped on exactly the boxes the build **produces**, and
the resolve's output set is promoted to it after the fact. So a check bed's
`--tag check-comfyui-pod-<calver>` no longer renames `arch-builder`, and the emitted Containerfile
no longer names `ghcr.io/opencharly/arch-builder:check-comfyui-pod-<calver>` — a ref no registry
serves, which died as
`failed to resolve source metadata: … not found` even though the image was in the local store
under its own tag (opencharly/charly#1208). DESIGN.md **D-KIND-2** — vocabulary data is nodes in
the repositories that own it — is the requirement this obeys: a caller's tag has no business
renaming another repository's node.

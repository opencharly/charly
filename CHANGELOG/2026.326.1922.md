## Summary

**Commit 1 (`93d1e4b`): aur is a builder like npm.** The embedded vocabulary (`charly/charly.yml`, `charly/testdata/build.yml`):
- the arch distro's `format.aur` is deleted, so there is no second install road (`pacman -U` from a format template) and no `secondary` format or its cache mount;
- `builder.aur` drops `detect_config`, because a candy declares its aur packages under a distro tag and the image honours them when its tag chain selects the tag — the authored builder node gains nothing;
- the aur host template stages into the candy's own package directory (the plugin-declared `{{.PkgDir}}`) and fails when `yay` built nothing, in place of the `|| true` search across three caches.

Its supporting changes:
- `charly/embed_defaults.go` gains `embeddedDefaultsDigest()`, `charly/loader_threaded.go` stamps `lp.EmbeddedDefaultsDigest`, and `charly/embed_defaults_digest_test.go` pins it — the materialized-tree cache key must change when the embedded vocabulary does;
- `tools/golden-compile/main.go` follows the new builder input, and `charly/testdata/deploy_compile_parity_golden.json` (new `reverse_ops`/`packages`) plus `deploy_compile_parity_tags.json` record it;
- nine test files follow the vocabulary: `apk_host_install_cell_test.go`, `build_target_oci_test.go`, `candy_test_helpers_test.go`, `distro_cascade_test.go`, `embed_defaults_test.go`, `format_config_test.go`, `install_plan_test.go`, `layers_test.go`, `validate_fixture_test.go`.

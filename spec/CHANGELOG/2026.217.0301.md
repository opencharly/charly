  inferring it from mtime/fd-visibility.

  label-family candidate set is filtered on the requested short name (the run-2 cross-bed
  image-crossing fix, D2).
- `schema`: `#CandyService` + `#ServiceRenderContext` gain `exec_start_pre`/`exec_start_post`
